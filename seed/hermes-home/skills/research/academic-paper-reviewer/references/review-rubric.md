# Review Rubric

Use these dimensions as a stable spine for review output:

- `Originality and contribution`: Is there a clear and non-trivial contribution?
- `Methodological soundness`: Are design, sampling, measures, and inference defensible?
- `Evidence support`: Do claims match the cited evidence, tables, and figures?
- `Structure and argument`: Does the manuscript progress coherently from question to conclusion?
- `Presentation quality`: Clarity, terminology discipline, tables/figures, references, formatting.

## Verdict Mapping

- `Accept`: no material threats to validity or publishability
- `Minor revision`: strong core, localized fixes needed
- `Major revision`: substantial fixes needed, but salvageable
- `Reject`: the central contribution or evidence base is not currently defensible

## Severity Heuristic

- `Critical`: blocks trust in the central result or contribution
- `Major`: materially weakens publishability
- `Minor`: improves clarity, precision, or packaging
